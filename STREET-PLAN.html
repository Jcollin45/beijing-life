A02The junction — enlarged

Where the whole plan turned, drawn as built. The corner block already stood at x 12.0–23.4 with a blank east elevation onto the road; 药店 and 银行 are on it now, and the district has its first corner — hardware facing the hutong, civic facing the road — for no new mass at all. Amber is what was drawn here and not built.

Two things the drawing got wrong, and building it settled. STREET-BLUEPRINT.md §4.1 put the west-side frontage at x ≈ 26.9, which is hard against the kerb face at 27.58. The building line is x 23.30 — the corner block's own east elevation, already standing as blank wall. And the road zone's x0 did not need to move: it starts at 24.0, clampMove holds the body at 24.30, and the 0.88 m in front of the glass is the same unreachable strip the alley's shopfronts stand in — which is why none of the new frontage carries a collider. The shelter went to x 26.15, not 26.50; at 26.50 its glass would have crossed the kerb face. 五金电器 does not move at all: it is the corner's other face.

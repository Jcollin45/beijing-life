怎么玩

How to play

You just arrived. ¥260, a backpack, a phone, and a small apartment in a 朝阳区 tower. Walk around. Click things. The whole building is your first classroom — every object here has a name, and the more you meet a word, the less the game translates it for you.

Words come back. A gold dot means one is asking to be recalled: answer it and it fades further out of English. Get it wrong and it returns sooner.

And you can leave. Step through your red 门, take the 电梯 down to 一楼, then leave through the 大堂 doors. Outside is the 胡同 — grey brick walls, courtyard gates, a breakfast stall, the corner 超市, and the towers of the city standing in the smog at the end of the road. The subway runs from there to the campus, the station and the airport.

You also have to live here. 吃饭, 厕所, 睡觉, 干净, 心情 drain as the day passes — press Q to actually use what you are standing next to. Eat at the fridge, sleep in the bed, work at the computer for money.
